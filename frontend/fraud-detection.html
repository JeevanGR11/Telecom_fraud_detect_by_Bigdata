Fraud Detection Engine

Hadoop MapReduce Distributed Analytics Pipeline
Distributed Pipeline

Hadoop MapReduce Job Submission

IDLE

Triggering this workflow executes the compiled Apache Hadoop MapReduce driver (CDRFraudMapper & CDRFraudReducer). The distributed mapper partitions records across unique Indian caller MSISDN keys, while the reducer computes calling frequency, distinct receivers, short-duration ping ratios (Wangiri), and persists calculated risk scores directly to MySQL.
